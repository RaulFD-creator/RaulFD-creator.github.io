I'm an Industrial PhD Student at [University College Dublin - Conway Institute for Biomolecular and Biomedical Research](https://www.ucd.ie/conway/) and [IBM Research Dublin](https://research.ibm.com/labs/ireland), where I research ML algorithms for bioinformatics. Specifically, my main focus is on self-supervised multimodal foundation models for protein and peptide representation learning. My thesis supevisors are [Denis Shields](https://people.ucd.ie/denis.shields) and [Thanh Lam Hoang](https://research.ibm.com/people/thanh-hoang).

Previously, I completed a BSc. in Biotechnology at the [University of Oviedo](https://insilichem.com/) and a MSc. in Bioinformatics (with focus on Structural Bioinformatics) at the [Autonomous University of Barcelona](https://www.uab.cat/web/universitat-autonoma-de-barcelona-1345467954774.html). After my Master's I worked as a Research Assistant at the [Insilichem Research Group](https://insilichem.com/)
